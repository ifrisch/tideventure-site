Settings

Firm-wide defaults used across the admin panel.

Scheduling Link

Paste your booking link (Calendly, Cal.com, Google Appointment Schedule, etc.). Clients get a Book a Call button on their portal dashboard. Leave blank to hide it.

Master Welcome Email

This is the default message shown whenever you generate a welcome link for a new client — you can still edit it per client before sending. Use these placeholders and they'll be filled in automatically: {{firstName}}, {{bizLine}}, {{businessName}}, {{setupUrl}}, {{email}}. The logo, signature, and footer are added automatically and shouldn't be included here.

Master Engagement Letter

Every new client reviews and signs this letter in their portal before their account activates. Placeholders are filled in per client automatically: {{clientName}}, {{businessName}}, {{date}}, {{email}}, {{servicesList}} (from their assigned services), and {{feeSchedule}} (from their plan's monthly/yearly pricing). Signed copies are stored with a timestamp, IP address, and document fingerprint, and the client gets a copy in their Documents tab.

Note: changes affect clients who haven't signed yet. Clients who already signed keep the exact letter text they agreed to, stored in their signing record.

Backups

All client data (documents, records, signed letters) is copied nightly to a separate backup bucket. Backups are additive — deleting something from the live system never deletes it from the backup.
