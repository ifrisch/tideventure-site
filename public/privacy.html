Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, and protect your information.

Last updated: August 2026

TideVenture CPA ("we," "us," "our firm") is committed to protecting the privacy and confidentiality of the personal and financial information entrusted to us. This notice describes what information we collect through this website and our client portal, how we use and protect it, and the rights you have. Because we are a CPA firm, our obligations go well beyond a typical website privacy policy — they are grounded in federal law and professional standards, as described below.

Our Professional and Legal Obligations

As a provider of tax and accounting services, our firm is a "financial institution" under the Gramm-Leach-Bliley Act (GLBA), and our handling of your information is governed by several overlapping regimes:

  • Internal Revenue Code §7216. Federal law makes it a crime for a tax return preparer to use or disclose tax return information for any purpose other than preparing your return, without your specific written consent. We treat this as the foundation of how client information is handled.
  • The FTC Safeguards Rule (16 C.F.R. Part 314), which requires us to maintain administrative, technical, and physical safeguards for customer information.
  • The AICPA Code of Professional Conduct and applicable state board of accountancy rules, which prohibit disclosure of confidential client information without consent.
  • Federally authorized tax practitioner privilege (IRC §7525), under which certain tax advice communications may be legally privileged.

Information We Collect

  • Nonpublic personal and financial information (clients). In the course of an engagement we collect the information needed to perform our services — which may include names, addresses, Social Security or tax identification numbers, income and financial account information, tax documents, accounting records, and questionnaire responses. We collect this directly from you, through the secure client portal, and from third parties you authorize (for example, QuickBooks Online).
  • Contact and inquiry information (prospective clients). When you submit our contact form or pricing estimator, we collect the details you choose to provide — such as your name, email address, phone number, business entity type, and message — so we can respond.
  • Technical information. Our hosting infrastructure logs basic technical data (such as IP address and browser type) for security and reliability purposes. Portal activity such as document uploads, downloads, and electronic signatures is logged with timestamps to maintain the integrity of client records.

Cookies

This website does not use advertising or third-party analytics cookies, and we do not track you across other sites. A small number of strictly necessary cookies may be set by our hosting provider (Cloudflare) for security purposes, and signing in to the client portal uses session storage that is cleared when you close your browser. Because we use only strictly necessary cookies, no cookie consent banner is required.

How We Use Your Information

We use client information solely to provide the services you have engaged us for: preparing returns, performing accounting and advisory work, communicating about deadlines and engagement matters, billing, and maintaining the security and integrity of our systems and records. Consistent with IRC §7216, we do not use tax return information for any other purpose without your specific written consent. We use prospective-client inquiry information only to respond and follow up.

Information Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your information — to anyone, ever — and we do not share it with third parties for their marketing purposes. We do not disclose nonpublic personal information about current or former clients to nonaffiliated third parties, except:

  • As you direct or authorize in writing — for example, sending records to your attorney, lender, or a successor accountant;
  • To service providers that help us operate our practice (such as secure hosting, e-signature, and accounting software integrations you connect), who may process information solely to provide their services and are bound to protect it;
  • As required or permitted by law — such as in response to a validly issued subpoena or summons, or to comply with professional standards. If we receive a request for disclosure of potentially privileged information, we will notify you before responding where the law permits.

Consistent with federal law, we will not disclose your tax return information outside the United States, or to another preparer for a second opinion, without first receiving your written consent.

How We Protect Your Information

We maintain safeguards designed to meet the FTC Safeguards Rule, including:

  • Encryption in transit. All traffic to this website and the client portal is encrypted (HTTPS/TLS).
  • Encryption at rest. Documents and questionnaire responses uploaded to the client portal are encrypted using AES-256 encryption before storage.
  • Access controls. Client information is accessible only to authorized firm personnel; administrative systems are protected by multi-factor authentication.
  • Activity logging. Access to and activity within the portal is logged and auditable.
  • Secure transmission practices. We ask clients to exchange sensitive documents through the encrypted portal rather than email attachments.

No system can be guaranteed absolutely secure, but we apply safeguards appropriate to a firm entrusted with sensitive financial data, and we review them on an ongoing basis. In the event of a data incident affecting your information, we will notify you as required by applicable law.

Former Clients

Our confidentiality obligations do not end when an engagement does. We apply the same policies and safeguards to former clients' information as we do to current clients'.

Record Retention

We retain engagement records for the period described in your engagement letter and as required by professional standards and applicable law. We do not retain your original source documents; these are returned to you upon completion of each engagement. Inquiry information from prospective clients who do not engage us is kept only as long as needed to respond and follow up.

Your Rights and Choices

You may request access to or correction of your personal information at any time. You may also request deletion, subject to our legal and professional record-keeping obligations — certain records must be retained under IRS rules, state board requirements, and professional standards even after a deletion request. To make any request, email hello@tideventurecpa.com.

Children

This website and our services are directed at businesses and adults, not children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to This Notice

We may update this notice from time to time. The "last updated" date above reflects the most recent revision. For clients, material changes to how we handle your information will be communicated directly.

Contact

Questions about this notice or how your information is handled? Email us at hello@tideventurecpa.com — privacy questions come straight to the firm's principal, not a mailbox no one reads.
