Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How much do your services cost?

We believe in transparent pricing. Our pricing estimator gives you a ballpark range in under a minute — no email required. A firm quote comes after a free strategy session, once we understand your specific situation.

I already have an accountant. Is switching a hassle?

It's simpler than most owners expect. Once you authorize us, we handle the transfer of your records and prior returns directly. The cleanest time to switch is after a filing season or at a quarter end, but we can onboard you at any point in the year.

Do you only prepare tax returns?

No — filing is just one piece. We work with clients year-round: quarterly tax planning and estimated payments, monthly financial statements and review, and annual returns. The goal is that nothing about your taxes is ever a surprise.

Do we need to meet in person?

Not unless you want to. Documents, questionnaires, and signatures all move through our secure client portal, and we meet by phone or video. We can work with you wherever you are.

What types of businesses do you work with?

S-Corps, C-Corps, partnerships, multi- and single-member LLCs, and sole proprietors. Our focus is small business owners who want a real relationship with their CPA.

When is the best time to reach out?

Earlier than you think. The sooner we start — especially before year-end — the more planning opportunities we can act on. That said, a free strategy session is worthwhile at any point in the year.

How secure is my financial information?

Security is built into how we work. Everything moves through our secure client portal over an encrypted connection — never as email attachments — and documents you upload are encrypted at rest, accessible only to you and our firm. We never sell your information or share it for marketing. As a CPA firm, we're also bound by professional confidentiality obligations that go beyond a typical privacy policy — see our Privacy Policy for details.
